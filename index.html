Regulatory Framework // Evaluated Statutes & Rules FDCPA & CFPB Reg F
12 CFR § 1006.6(b)(1) Permissible Calling Hours Prohibits collection calls before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM in the borrower's local time zone.
12 CFR § 1006.14(b)(2) Call Frequency (7-in-7 Rule) Limits calls to a maximum of 7 telephone attempts within 7 consecutive rolling calendar days.
15 U.S.C. § 1692e(11) / § 807(11) Mini-Miranda Statutory Warning Mandates disclosing in initial communication that caller is collecting a debt and info will be used for that purpose.
15 U.S.C. § 1692c(b) / § 805(b) Third-Party Privacy Barrier Strictly forbids discussing debt balance or delinquency with spouses, family, or third parties without consent.
15 U.S.C. § 1692c(c) / § 805(c) Cease Communication Right Requires immediate cessation of calls and payment pressure upon consumer oral or written request to stop.
15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b) / § 809(b) Disputed Debt Validation Pause Mandates pausing all collection upon dispute until formal written validation of the debt is mailed.
15 U.S.C. § 1692e(4)-(5) / § 807(4)-(5) Prohibited Legal & Garnishment Threats Bars deceptive threats of wage garnishment, asset seizure, or lawsuits that cannot legally be taken or intended.
